# about

Hi. This is a garage: a place to tinker, take things apart, and put them back together slightly better.

Lately that means **fine-tuning small LLMs on a student budget**, chasing faster training runs, and learning how serving actually works once more than one person shows up.

The rules of the garage:

- **ship the rough version.** polish comes later, or never.
- **measure, don't guess.** if it's faster, show the number.
- **cheap > fancy.** free tiers first, credits second, cards last.

Code lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/xenagarage). New posts come out via [RSS](/feed.xml).
